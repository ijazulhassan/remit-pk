Common questions

Why do exchange rates differ between providers?

Every provider buys currency at roughly the same wholesale "mid-market" rate, then adds its own margin before quoting you a price. Some, like Wise, show that margin as a separate flat fee and pass you the real rate. Others fold the margin into a worse exchange rate so the transfer looks "fee-free" — but you still pay for it, just less visibly.

Is it safe to send money through these apps?

Wise, Remitly and WorldRemit are all regulated money transfer businesses in the countries they operate in, and each processes millions of transfers a year. They're not banks, but they're licensed and monitored specifically for cross-border payments.

How often are these rates updated?

We refresh the numbers on this page regularly. Exchange rates move throughout the day, every day, so treat the figures here as a close guide to who's currently ahead — always confirm the exact quote on the provider's site before sending.

Bank transfer or cash pickup — which is cheaper?

Bank transfers are almost always cheaper because there's no cash-handling cost for the provider. Cash pickup costs more but gets money into a family member's hands the same day without them needing a bank account.
